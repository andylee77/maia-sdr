# Fishball P25 — Address Map (Single Source of Truth)

This document is the canonical reference for **all** addressable resources in
the Fishball P25 IP core: DDR carve-outs for DMA ring buffers, AXI-Lite
register banks, and IRQ assignments. It is the file every future phase
updates first, **before** any wiring code lands. Code docstrings link back
here rather than duplicating the table.

If the live gateware ever disagrees with this document, the gateware is
correct and this document is wrong — fix the document.

## DDR carve-outs (DMA ring buffers)

All ring buffers live in a reserved DDR region carved out by the kernel /
device tree (Phase 6D will pin the exact carve-out range). Each ring base
must be aligned to its **total ring size** (this is asserted by
`P25Config.validate()`).

| Name | Base | Sub-buffers | Sub-buffer size | Total | Ring depth | IRQ rate | Byte rate | Source |
|------|------|-------------|-----------------|-------|------------|----------|-----------|--------|
| `dibit_dma`   | `0x1700_0000` | 8 | 4 KB    | 32 KB   | ~25 s    | ~3.2 s   | ~1.28 KB/s   | control-channel dibits (post-slicer, post-symbol-timing) |
| `traffic_dma` | `0x1800_0000` | 8 | 4 KB    | 32 KB   | ~25 s    | ~3.2 s   | ~1.28 KB/s   | traffic-channel dibits (post-slicer, post-symbol-timing) |
| `iq_dma`      | `0x1900_0000` | 8 | 32 KB   | 256 KB  | ~1 s     | ~128 ms  | ~250 KB/s    | **Phase 6C:** control-channel post-DDC IQ (16-bit signed I + 16-bit signed Q, 62.5 kSPS, two samples per 64-bit DMA word) |

**Sample-rate math (control DDC at 62.5 kSPS):**

```text
62.5 kSPS x (16+16 bits) = 250 KB/s
250 KB/s / 32 KB sub-buffer = ~7.8 IRQ/s = ~128 ms per sub-buffer
8 sub-buffers x 32 KB = 256 KB ring = ~1.0 s of IQ in flight
```

**Why these specific values for `iq_dma`:**

- **256 KB total** is large enough that the PS does not need to keep up
  in real time at sub-buffer granularity — even a sluggish Cortex-A9
  Python prototype run can drain a full second of IQ between polls.
- **8 sub-buffers** mirrors the dibit / traffic ring layout exactly so
  the existing `last_buffer` / `next_address` debug semantics carry
  over without surprises.
- **32 KB per sub-buffer** keeps the per-IRQ work small (~32 K bytes
  per interrupt) — much smaller than that would IRQ-flood the PS at
  IQ rates; much larger would defeat the point of having sub-buffers.
- **`0x1900_0000`** continues the existing `0x17/0x18/0x19` carve-out
  pattern and is naturally aligned to its 256 KB ring size.

**IQ packing format (per 64-bit DMA word):**

```text
bit 63                                                              bit 0
+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+
| im[1] high     | im[1] low      | re[1] high     | re[1] low      |  ← sample 1 (later in time)
+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+
| im[0] high     | im[0] low      | re[0] high     | re[0] low      |  ← sample 0 (earlier)
+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+--------+
       63..48           47..32           31..16           15..0
```

Sample 0 is in the low half. Each sample is `{im[15:0], re[15:0]}` with
`re` in the low 16 bits and `im` in the high 16 bits, both 16-bit signed
two's complement matching `DDC.re_out` / `DDC.im_out`. The PS-side reader
should interpret each 64-bit word as four little-endian `int16` values in
the order `re0, im0, re1, im1`.

## AXI-Lite register banks

The AXI-Lite slave is a 7-bit word-addressed bus (= 9-bit byte address,
512-byte BAR). The bank decoder uses bits **[5:3]** of the word address
(3 bits → 8 banks max), and bits **[2:0]** select the register within a
bank (3 bits → 8 registers per bank max). Banks are 0x20 bytes apart.

The IP-XACT core lives at base address **`0x7C46_0000`** in the Zynq GP0
window (set by `ad_cpu_interconnect 0x7C460000 p25_core` in `system_bd.tcl`).

| Bank | Word base | Byte base (PS view) | Name | Reg-field bits | Registers used / max | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------------------|------|----------------|----------------------|---------|
| 0 | `0x00` | `0x7C46_0000` | `control` | 2 | 4 / 4 | product_id, version, control (sdr_reset), interrupts |
| 1 | `0x08` | `0x7C46_0020` | `sdr` | 3 | 5 / 8 | control DDC: coeff_addr, coeff, decimation, frequency, control |
| 2 | `0x10` | `0x7C46_0040` | `demod` | 2 | 3 / 4 | demod_status, demod_control, dibit_next_address |
| 3 | `0x18` | `0x7C46_0060` | `traffic` | 3 | 6 / 8 | traffic DDC + traffic demod + traffic_next_address |
| 4 | `0x20` | `0x7C46_0080` | `iq` (Phase 6C) | 2 | 3 / 4 | iq_dma_status, iq_dma_control, iq_next_address |
| 5 | `0x28` | `0x7C46_00A0` | *(free)* | — | — | next available bank |
| 6 | `0x30` | `0x7C46_00C0` | *(free)* | — | — | |
| 7 | `0x38` | `0x7C46_00E0` | *(free)* | — | — | last bank in the 7-bit word-address space |

### `iq` bank (Phase 6C) detail — byte offsets relative to `0x7C46_0080`

| Byte offset | Register | Field | Bits | Access | Description |
|-------------|----------|-------|------|--------|-------------|
| `0x00` | `iq_dma_status`  | `iq_overflow` | `[0]`    | Rsticky | latches when `IQPacker.data_valid` asserts while `iq_dma.stream_ready` is low (i.e. AXI back-pressure starved the packer); cleared on read |
| `0x00` | `iq_dma_status`  | `last_buffer` | `[19:16]`* | R       | index of most recently completed sub-buffer (mirrors `iq_dma.last_buffer`); width = `iq_dma_num_buffers_log2` = 3 bits |
| `0x04` | `iq_dma_control` | `iq_enable`   | `[0]`    | RW      | enable bit for the ring DMA's AW channel; mirrors the `dibit_dma` enable convention (level signal, not pulse) |
| `0x08` | `iq_next_address`| `next_address`| `[31:0]` | R       | current AW write address inside the ring (debug only) |

*`last_buffer` is laid out at bits [16+:N] to leave the low half free for
future status flags, matching the `demod_status` and `traffic_demod_status`
layout convention.

## IRQ assignments

The P25 IP exposes a single `interrupt_out` line that is the OR of all
sticky interrupt bits in `control.interrupts`. It is connected to
`sys_concat_intc/In11` in the Vivado block design.

| Bit | Name (in `control.interrupts`) | Source signal | Purpose |
|-----|-------------------------------|---------------|---------|
| 0 | `dibit_dma`   | `dibit_dma.interrupt`   | sub-buffer of control-channel dibit ring filled |
| 1 | `traffic_dma` | `traffic_dma.interrupt` | sub-buffer of traffic-channel dibit ring filled |
| 2 | `iq_dma` (Phase 6C) | `iq_dma.interrupt` | sub-buffer of control-channel IQ ring filled |

All bits are `Rsticky` — they latch on the source pulse and clear on read.

## HP-port wiring

The Zynq HP1 slave port hosts all three DMA masters via Vivado SmartConnect
(extended idempotently by repeated `ad_mem_hp1_interconnect` calls):

| AXI master | HP slave | Sustained byte rate | HP1 budget @ 1.7 GB/s |
|------------|----------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| `m_axi_dibit`   | HP1 | ~1.28 KB/s | <0.001% |
| `m_axi_traffic` | HP1 | ~1.28 KB/s | <0.001% |
| `m_axi_iq`      | HP1 | ~250 KB/s  | ~0.015% |

HP1 is wildly overprovisioned for these consumers; HP2/HP3 are unused and
remain available for future high-bandwidth needs (e.g. wideband recorder).

## Future-self checklist when adding a new register bank or DMA

1. Update this document **first** — pick the bank/address and write down
   the *why*.
2. Update `P25Config` (and its `validate()`) for any new DDR carve-out.
3. Add the new module + `Registers` block + `RegisterMap` entry in
   `p25_top.py`. Mirror the `dibit` / `traffic` / `iq` patterns.
4. **Widen the bank decoder** if you cross a power-of-two boundary
   (currently `address[3:6]` = 3 bits = 8 banks; the slice and the
   read-mux already cover all 8 slots).
5. Extend the `RegisterCDC` instances and the `rdata`/`rdone`/`wdone`
   OR-tree in `elaborate()` for any new sync-domain register bank.
6. Add the new AXI master to `package_ip.tcl` (`ipx::associate_bus_interfaces`)
   and `system_bd.tcl` (`ad_mem_hp1_interconnect`).
7. Regenerate `p25_core.v` via `build_hdl.bat --verilog-only --p25` and
   verify the staleness check trips on a subsequent `build_fpga.bat --p25`.
8. Write a `doc/changes/NNN_*.md` change doc and update `DEVLOG.md` /
   `CHANGELOG_FORK.md` once the bitstream builds and on-hardware smoke
   passes.
